JazakAllah for using Muhasaba.

• Challenges — set yourself a goal like 40 nights of Tahajjud or 1000 salawat, and see whether you're on pace. If you fall behind, the app tells you.
• Insights — History and Stats are now one tab: streaks, a consistency grid, and progress for every amal.
• Monthly acts can fall on several dates, with a quota per period.
• A short walkthrough the first time you open the app.
• Stopped acts can be found and restored.
