JazakAllah for using Muhasaba.

• Fixed a crash when saving an option set with a long name or option — most often in Bengali, Urdu, Arabic and Hindi. Length limits now count the characters you see, so every language gets the same room.

Also new in 3.0:
• Record how an amal was done, not only whether: Fajr alone or in jamaa at the masjid, Quran recited or memorised, sadaqah as money or time. Use the ready-made sets or create your own.
• New "Any" goal: no target — any amount marks the day done.
• Insights shows how the choices split, amal by amal, with trends and records across the year.
• Support the app with a tip if you like. Nothing is locked behind it: Muhasaba stays free, with no ads and no account.
