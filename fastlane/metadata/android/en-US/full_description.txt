Muhasaba is a private daily tracker for the acts of worship you already want to keep — salah, dhikr, Qur'an, sadaqah and your own sunnah habits.

Named after muhāsaba (محاسبة), the practice of taking account of oneself, it turns your intentions into a simple daily checklist you'll actually keep. No account, no sign-up, and nothing to configure before you start.

WHAT YOU TRACK
• Daily, weekly and monthly acts — from the five daily prayers to a weekly fast or monthly charity
• Count-based goals like 33x tasbih or a page of Qur'an, with a quick tap counter
• Your own custom acts and categories, each with an emoji that suits it

CHALLENGES
Set yourself a goal and see it through — 40 nights of Tahajjud, 1000 salawat, a khatm in 30 days. Muhasaba works out the pace you need, shows whether you're on track, and nudges you the day you fall behind.

INSIGHTS
• Streaks for every amal, and for your whole day
• A consistency grid showing each day you kept
• Completion rate by category and by act, over any period
• Revisit any past day, add a private note, or record a day you missed

MADE TO FEEL EFFORTLESS
• A clean Today screen — a simple list, or grouped by category
• Daily reminders at a time you choose
• Light and dark themes
• Adjustable day-start hour and start of week
• 23 languages with full right-to-left support, including English, العربية, اردو, বাংলা, Bahasa Indonesia, Türkçe and فارسی

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your tracked deeds are stored only on your device. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no ads, and the app works fully offline. Anonymous crash and usage statistics help us fix problems; they contain nothing about your worship.
