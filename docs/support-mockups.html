0 · The badge ladder

You set $19.99 = Ansar (helper). The other three follow the same idea — a word for someone who stands with you, rising in weight. All four are everyday given names across every one of the app's 23 language communities, so each translator knows the local spelling on sight (Ensar / Anshar / আনসার / انصار) and nothing gets "translated" into a paraphrase. The icons grow with the tier; the palm at the top is a quiet nod to the date-farmers of Madinah.

Badge rule: highest tier you have ever tipped recommended

Tip $19.99 once → Ansar, for good. Repeat tips never lower it; a bigger tip raises it. Each tile on the sheet maps 1:1 to a title, so what you get is obvious before you tap.

Alternative: cumulative total

Tips add up in tier points (2 / 5 / 10 / 20); four $4.99 tips make an Ansar. Kinder to repeat tippers, but the sheet then wants a progress line ("$5 more to Muhsin") — and that is the collection-plate feel we are avoiding.

1 · The popup 30th active day

Appears once, on the Today tab, right after the completion that makes it the 30th active day. Three actions, always all visible.

2 · The tip sheet

Opened from the Settings row or from "Support now". Prices arrive from the store already in the user's currency — only the titles, meanings and copy are ours to translate.

3 · The Support section

One card at the top of Settings, there from day one, replacing both the row in the Support group and the hadith carousel. Before the first tip: app icon, name, the privacy line, the short copy, one button. After: the tier icon takes the app icon's place, then the badge line, one line of thanks, "Supporter since", and the same button as "Support again". The hadith survives as a single quiet line at the very bottom of Settings — a different one each time the screen opens, no carousel, no timer.
